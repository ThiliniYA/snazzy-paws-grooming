Special-Breed Cuts

Medicated Bath

For pets with skin conditions requiring special shampoo. An additional $20 – $40.

Recommended for dogs with itchy, sensitive or allergy-prone skin.

De-Shedding Treatment

Special shampoo and undercoat removal, added to a bath or haircut. An additional $20 – $40.

Ideal for heavy-shedding, double-coated breeds like Huskies and Labradors.
