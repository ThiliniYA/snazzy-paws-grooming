Brushing Tips for Long-Haired Breeds

Long-haired dogs benefit from brushing at least 3-4 times a week to prevent matting and tangles. Always brush in the direction of hair growth, and use a slicker brush for the undercoat followed by a comb to catch any remaining knots. Regular brushing between grooming visits keeps your dog's coat healthier and makes each salon visit quicker and more comfortable.

How Often Should You Bathe Your Dog?

Most dogs do well with a bath every 4-6 weeks, though this varies by breed, coat type and lifestyle. Bathing too often can strip natural oils and dry out the skin, while too infrequent bathing can lead to odour and matting. If your dog has a skin condition, ask us about our medicated bath option.

Signs Your Dog Needs a Grooming Visit

Watch for matted or tangled fur, overgrown nails that click on hard floors, a noticeable odour, or excessive shedding around the house. Ears that smell unusual or seem irritated are also worth a professional look. Regular grooming every 4-8 weeks helps catch these issues early.

Tips for Pet Owners

  • Introduce grooming tools early — let puppies sniff and get used to brushes before their first real session.
  • Reward calm behaviour during and after grooming with treats or praise, to build positive associations.
  • Check paws and ears weekly at home, even between salon visits, to catch problems early.
  • Keep up with regular nail trims — overgrown nails can affect your dog's posture and walking.
