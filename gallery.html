Brushing Tips for Long-Haired Breeds

Long-haired dogs benefit from brushing at least 3–4 times a week to prevent matting and tangles. Always brush in the direction of hair growth, using a slicker brush for the undercoat followed by a comb to catch remaining knots. Regular brushing keeps the coat healthier and makes grooming visits quicker and more comfortable.

How Often Should You Bathe Your Dog?

Most dogs do well with a bath every 4–6 weeks, though this varies by breed, coat type and lifestyle. Bathing too often can strip natural oils, while infrequent bathing can lead to odour and matting. If your dog has a skin condition, ask us about our medicated bath option.

Signs Your Dog Needs a Grooming Visit

Watch for matted fur, overgrown nails that click on hard floors, noticeable odour, or excessive shedding. Ears that smell unusual or seem irritated also need a professional look. Regular grooming every 4–8 weeks helps catch issues early.

Tips for Pet Owners

  • 🐾 Introduce grooming tools early — let puppies sniff and get used to brushes before their first session.
  • 🐾 Reward calm behaviour during and after grooming to build positive associations.
  • 🐾 Check paws and ears weekly to catch problems early, even between salon visits.
  • 🐾 Keep up with regular nail trims — overgrown nails can affect posture and walking.
